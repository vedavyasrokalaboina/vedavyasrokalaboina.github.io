Group Project · Team of 3

Business Jet Design, Analysis & Simulation

Sep 2024 – Mar 2025 8 passengers MATLAB · SolidWorks · Merlin Simulator
Overview

As part of a three-person team, I contributed to the conceptual design of an eight-passenger business jet, performing a complete preliminary design process to estimate the aircraft's overall weight, geometry, propulsion requirements, and flight performance from first-principles calculation — not copied from an existing aircraft.

The process began with a take-off weight calculation built from mission requirements — passenger capacity, fuel fraction, payload, and range — and everything downstream depended on getting that right. From there, we determined wing loading, thrust-to-weight ratio, and aerodynamic configuration aimed at efficient cruise performance and stable flight behaviour.

Methodology
  • Numerical sizing — MATLAB scripts to calculate wingspan, wing area, fuselage length, tail sizing, and required engine thrust across take-off, climb, cruise, and landing.
  • CAD modelling — a detailed 3D SolidWorks assembly representing the full external configuration: fuselage, wing, empennage, and propulsion integration.
  • Structural & aerodynamic checks — SolidWorks Simulation to evaluate structural behaviour under aerodynamic loading, keeping the design light without giving up structural integrity.
  • Flight validation — testing the finished design in the Merlin flight simulator across take-off, cruise, manoeuvring, and landing, iterating across three versions to refine performance.
Result

The simulation results showed stable flight behaviour with only minimal performance deviations across every regime tested — not just a CAD model that looked right, but one that flew the way the calculations said it should.
