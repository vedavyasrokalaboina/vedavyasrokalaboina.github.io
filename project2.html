SAE Aero Design Competition

Apr 2022 – Feb 2024 ~200-inch wingspan Chennai → Orlando, Florida
Overview

As part of a small, multi-disciplinary student team at ARDC, IARE, I contributed to the design and development of a large-scale radio-controlled aircraft for the SAE Aero Design Competition, organised by SAE International. The project spanned the full design cycle — conceptual configuration, aerofoil selection, aerodynamic optimisation, weight estimation, and structural reinforcement of the components that mattered most for keeping a ~200-inch wingspan aircraft intact under real flight loads.

The project first achieved national-level recognition at the SAE Aero Design National Championship in Chennai, India, which qualified the team for the SAE Aero Design International Competition in Orlando, Florida, USA.

My Contribution
  • Aerodynamic analysis — computational estimation of lift, drag, and stability across different flight conditions to inform the configuration.
  • Structural evaluation — assessing whether the airframe could survive take-off, cruise, and landing loads without carrying unnecessary weight.
  • CAD modelling — wing, fuselage, landing gear system, and propulsion mounting, using SolidWorks and CATIA V5, making sure every sub-assembly agreed geometrically before manufacture.
  • Design review — helping compile the technical evidence base and contributing to the formal design review presented before appraisal panels at both competitions.
Result

Competing against international teams from leading engineering institutions, the aircraft demonstrated strong aerodynamic efficiency and structural stability during flight evaluation in Florida, securing a 15th place finish globally out of the full international field.
