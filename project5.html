RC Plane Modelling and Flight Test

Introductory Aeromodelling Project Best Project Prize
Overview

This was my first aircraft — the very first time I took something from a drawing to an object that actually left the ground under its own power. It was an introductory aeromodelling project during my Bachelor's degree, and the brief was simple to say and considerably less simple to do: design, build, and successfully fly a stable radio-controlled aircraft.

Every RC aircraft I'd seen fly before this one belonged to someone with years of experience. Mine belonged to me, and I had no idea whether the control surfaces were sized correctly or whether the centre of gravity was actually where the calculations said it should be.

The Story

Design & build

Designed and built the aircraft from scratch as an introductory aeromodelling project — sizing, construction, and balancing done for the first time, with no prior hands-on build experience to fall back on.

First flight

The aircraft held a stable, continuous flight and came back down to a safe, controlled landing — for a first attempt, nothing short of a triumph.

Best Project Prize

The project went on to win the Best Project Prize at the university for its successful design and flight demonstration.

The door it opened

This project is what got me into ARDC — the Aero Research and Development Club — where I went on to work on far more advanced aircraft design and aeromodelling projects, including the SAE Aero Design competition.
